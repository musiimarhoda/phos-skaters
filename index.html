Quick answers

Before you ask.

How far ahead should we book?

As early as you can. Two weeks gives us room to plan outfits, routes and the routine. Last-minute? Message us anyway and we'll see what's possible.

Can the crew wear our brand colours?

Yes. Branded tees, caps, sashes or full outfits. Tell us what you have, or we can sort it for you.

Do you only work in Kampala?

Kampala is home, but we travel. Tell us where the event is and we'll plan around it.

Can you film it too?

Phos Skaters is part of Phos Creatives, so photo and video can come in the same package.
